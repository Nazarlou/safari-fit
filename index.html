THE FINER DETAILS

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Is this a private safari?

Our concept is independent travel: your own party and a tailored itinerary, rather than a scheduled group tour. Some lodge activities or transfers may be shared; these would be specified in a confirmed proposal.

Can I change one of the suggested journeys?

Absolutely. Choose a journey as a starting point, then adjust your days, budget, travelers, style, and interests in the planner.

What do the sample prices include?

Demo estimates assume accommodation, private guiding, game drives, park fees, and ground transfers. International flights, insurance, visas, gratuities, and optional activities are excluded. A real quote must specify every inclusion.

Can I book or pay on this site?

Not in this preview. You can explore packages, create a sample itinerary, and download a trip brief. Prices, accommodation, and availability are illustrative.

Is the concierge a live AI service?

This version uses a simulated concierge to demonstrate the experience. It can recognize a few travel preferences and use your planning form, but it does not access live inventory or provide confirmed advice.

What happens to my information?

Your trip and chat stay in this page’s memory and disappear when you reload. The demo contact form does not send or save your details. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives ordinary web request information.
